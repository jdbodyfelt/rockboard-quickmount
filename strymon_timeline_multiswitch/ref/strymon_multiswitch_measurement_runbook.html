Original Strymon MultiSwitch — measurement runbook

All dimensions in mm. Record to 0.1 mm where calipers permit.

Before measuring

Use digital calipers, measure each critical feature twice, and write the two readings in the note if they differ. This sheet records only the bottom face used by the mounting plate: the lower tray and its four assembly screws.

Do not use the four base screws as mounting points yet. They are assembly screws. Do not remove more than one at once, and do not tighten a screw beyond its original engagement depth.

1 · 2D lower-tray footprint and base-screw pattern

For the mounting plate, use only the external bottom face of the lower tray and the four base-screw centres. Ignore internal channel returns and the upper shell. Put the unit bottom-up; this sketch is that view.
