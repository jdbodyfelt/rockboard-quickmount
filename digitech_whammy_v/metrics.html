How to use this sheet

Every field wants millimetres, to 0.1 mm. Use the calipers' jaws for outside and inside spans, and the depth rod for heights. Where a number is awkward to reach, put your best reading in the value box and say why in the note - a flagged approximation is far more use to me than a blank.

Why this exists. The manual's Page 2 drawing is the right shape, but its printed proportions are deliberately compressed, so none of its dimensions can be trusted. The drawing tells me where a feature is and on which face; only your calipers can tell me how big it is. Nothing in the model will be copied from the manual.

Two photographs are worth ten numbers. The top face shot square-on from directly above with a steel rule lying on it; and one long side shot square-on with the rule along it. Send those and I can cross-check every reading against them.

Entries save in this browser as you type. Copy JSON puts the whole set on the clipboard - paste it back and I will build the config from it.

0 · Set the datum

Everything else is measured from two named faces, so name them once, here. From page 2 of the manual: the POWER barrel jack and the OUTPUT jack share one long side; INPUT and MIDI share the other. Those are the only fixed names I will use.

IDWhat to measureYour reading
D1 must

Run your finger along each long side. One has a 9 V barrel jack and a 1/4" output; the other has a 1/4" input and a 5-pin MIDI. Record which is which as seen when the footswitch is nearest you.
D2 must

That end is the toe end throughout this sheet. The treadle tips down toward it, and its pivot is at the far end.
D3 must

Confirm: toe end = footswitch end, hinge end = the opposite one, where the treadle's pivot bracket is visible.

1 · The body box

The outer shell below the top plate. Measure across the walls, not across the top plate - the top plate overhangs and will give you a bigger number. Take each reading twice, at two different places along the side, and put the worst pair in the note if they disagree.

IDWhat to measureYour reading
A1 must

Calipers across the body at about 10 mm up from the bottom, jaws square to the walls. This is the widest point of the lower shell.
A2 must

Same height, but jaws on the two end walls.
A3 helpful

Repeat A1 as high as you can get the jaws, immediately below where the top plate starts. If this differs from A1, the walls are tapered and I need both.
A4 helpful

Repeat A2 as high as possible.
A5 must

Depth rod of the calipers from the bottom face (not the rubber feet) up to the flat rim of the top plate, at mid-length on a long side.
A6 helpful

If the top plate is not flat - a raised area over the button panel, a lip, a boss - give the bottom face to that highest point.
A7 optional

Optional. Lay the calipers across a corner and read the widest gap, or just photograph one corner square-on with a ruler in frame and I will scale it.

2 · The top plate and its overhang

The top plate overhangs the body on all four sides. Those overhangs are what a wall would have to tuck under, so they need to be real numbers rather than 'looks like a few mm'.

IDWhat to measureYour reading
B1 must

Calipers across the top plate's edge and the body wall directly below it. Jaws horizontal, measuring how far the plate sticks out.
B2 must

Same on the other long side.
B3 must

Same, but on the footswitch end.
B4 must

Same, on the opposite end.
B5 must

The plate's own edge thickness, measured on the overhanging brim.
B6 must

Under the overhanging brim, from the plate's underside down to the first thing below it - the body shoulder or the jack panel. This is the space an end wall would have to fit into.

3 · The five top-plate screws

These are the only fasteners on the pedal, and the whole mount hangs off them, so this section matters more than its length suggests. Photograph one head square-on with calipers on it, as well as filling in the numbers.

IDWhat to measureYour reading
C1 must

Count them. They are black hex-socket heads flush-ish with the top plate.
C2 must

Jaws on two opposite flats of the hexagon, not on the corners.
C3 helpful

Jaws on two opposite corners - if it reads the same as C2 the head is round; if it reads bigger, it is a hex.
C4 must

Depth rod, or the calipers' depth blade, from the plate's top face to the head's top face.
C5 helpful

Try your hex keys. Tell me the size that fits snugly.
C6 helpful

If the head sits in a recess, the plate's top face will be above the head.
C7 must

Wind one out and put the calipers on the thread's outside. This tells me M3 vs M2.5, which decides every screw in the mount.
C8 must

Same screw, from under the head to the tip.
C9 must

Lay the pedal flat, toe end toward you, side P on your left. With the calipers' depth rod, measure the screw centre from the side P wall and from the toe end wall, and give me both, 'x, y'.
C10 must

Same two references.
C11 must

Same two references, but x is measured from the side I wall.
C12 must

Same.
C13 helpful

Only if you counted 5 in C1 - the one that is not the four corners.

4 · The treadle — the section that decides the design

This is the part I got wrong last time, so it is worth slowing down here. I need the plate's true outline, where it sits relative to the body walls, and where it goes when you press it. Two photos taken square-on will help as much as the numbers: one at rest, one at full press, both with the calipers or a steel rule lying on the top plate.

IDWhat to measureYour reading
T1 must

The metal plate's own width, widest point. Do not include the rubber tread pad if it overhangs; give both if it does.
T2 must

Along the direction of travel.
T3 must

Calipers on the plate's side edge where you can reach it - at the toe end, just above the rubber bumper.
T4 must

The clear gap between the treadle's edge and the inside of the body wall on side P, measured at the toe end.
T5 must

Same on side I. If T4 and T5 differ, the treadle is off-centre - I need both.
T6 must

From the plate's toe edge to the inside of the body's end wall.
T7 helpful

From the plate's hinge-end edge to the body's end wall on that side. Usually hidden by the hinge bracket - get as close as you can and say so.
T8 must

Depth rod from the top plate's flat rim up to the treadle's top face, near the toe end.
T9 must

Press the treadle all the way down and hold it, then repeat T8 at the same point. It should be lower - possibly below the rim.
T10 must

Say yes or no, and by how much if yes. This decides whether anything can sit over the top face at all on that side.
T11 helpful

The pivot's centre, from the body's end wall. Look for the bracket or the axle boss.
T12 helpful

The pivot's centre height. Approximate is fine.
T13 helpful

If the treadle has a glued rubber pad on top, its thickness, and whether it overhangs the metal plate.

5 · The four jacks

Positions along each long side, and how far the hardware sticks out. Heights are from the bottom face. If a jack's centre is hard to judge, measure to the edge of its nut and say so - I can work from that.

IDWhat to measureYour reading
J1 must

Along side I.
J2 must

Vertical height of the jack's centreline.
J3 must

Along side I.
J4 must

Vertical.
J5 must

Along side P.
J6 must

Vertical.
J7 must

Along side P. Measure to the barrel socket's centre, not the wall wart's box.
J8 must

Vertical.
J9 must

From the side wall's face out to the nut's outer face. One number is enough if all four match.
J10 must

Barrel jacks often protrude more than the 1/4" ones.

6 · The feet, and the bottom face

The feet stay on the pedal - nothing gets removed - so the mount has to work around them. These four numbers set how the pedal sits on a plate.

IDWhat to measureYour reading
F1 must

Across one rubber pad.
F2 must

How far the pad stands proud of the bottom face.
F3 must

Are there four, one near each corner, or two rails, or something else? Say which, and give one pad's centre as 'x from side-P wall, y from toe end'.
F4 must

Any screws, plugs, ribs, or a recess. This is the one face the pedal rests on, so I need to know if it is interrupted.

7 · Where it sits on the board

Two questions about the RockBoard, so the QuickMount clips land on ribs rather than in slots. Look at the board with the pedal off it.

IDWhat to measureYour reading
R1 must

At the board's edge nearest where the pedal goes, look at the strip that runs along the edge: is the first thing a raised rib or an open slot?
R2 helpful

As you face the board, e.g. 'front right'.
